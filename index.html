    What the numbers say

    All thresholds are chosen on validation flows at a 1% false-positive target and then applied unchanged to untouched test flows, which keep their real benign/attack ratio. Splits are chronological with a purge gap, the graph only links a flow to earlier flows, and preprocessing is fitted on training data only.

    M.Sc. Computer Science final project, version 1. Data: NF-UNSW-NB15-v3, NF-CSE-CIC-IDS2018-v3 and NF-ToN-IoT-v3 (University of Queensland NetFlow v3 datasets).
